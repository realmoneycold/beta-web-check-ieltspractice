            # Raqobatchi Tahlili (IELTSPRACTICE) - Competitive Analysis
            ## Comprehensive Comparison with IELTS Preparation Platforms

            ---

            ## Executive Summary

            **Raqobatchi Tahlili** is a comprehensive IELTS preparation platform developed for the Uzbekistan market with global scalability potential. This analysis compares it against 10 major international competitors across key feature dimensions.

            ---

            ## 1. Platform Overview

            ### 1.1 Raqobatchi Tahlili (IELTSPRACTICE) - Your Platform

            **Target Market:** Uzbekistan (with global expansion capability)
            **Platform Type:** Full-featured IELTS preparation ecosystem
            **Unique Value Proposition:** AI-powered personalized learning with comprehensive multi-role management system

            **Core Features:**
            - **Multi-Role Architecture:** Student, Teacher, Admin, Centre Manager, CEO portals
            - **AI-Powered Tools:** AI Writing Assessment, AI Chat Mentor (Gemini/Groq integration)
            - **Complete Test Library:** 350+ practice tests across all 4 skills
            - **Gamification:** Typing Dojo, Leaderboards, Study Streaks, Achievement System
            - **Progress Tracking:** Detailed analytics, skill scoring (band 5.0-9.0), exam countdown
            - **Education Centre Network:** Physical centre integration with booking system
            - **Real-time Features:** WebRTC video calling for speaking practice

            ## 2. Competitor Analysis

            ### 2.1 British Council - Road to IELTS

            **Overview:** Official IELTS preparation from exam co-owner
            **Pricing:** Free (limited) / $50-100 (full version)
            **Target:** Global, all band levels

            **Key Features:**
            - 40+ official practice tests
            - 120+ hours of interactive activities
            - Expert video tutorials
            - Downloadable practice materials
            - Both Academic & General Training

            **Strengths:**
            - Official exam materials (authenticity)
            - British Council brand credibility
            - Comprehensive video content

            **Weaknesses:**
            - No AI-powered feedback
            - Limited personalization
            - No gamification elements
            - No live tutoring integration

            ### 2.3 Magoosh IELTS

            **Overview:** Premium online IELTS course platform
            **Pricing:** $109 - $129 (6 months access)
            **Target:** Self-motivated learners

            **Key Features:**
            - 80+ video lessons on strategies
            - 50+ grammar video lessons
            - 800+ practice questions
            - 10 full-length practice tests
            - Mobile apps (iOS/Android)
            - "Ask-An-Expert" feature
            - Score increase guarantee

            **Strengths:**
            - High-quality video explanations
            - Excellent mobile experience
            - Thorough answer explanations
            - Customizable study schedules

            **Weaknesses:**
            - No AI-powered writing assessment
            - No live speaking practice
            - No leaderboard/gamification
            - Academic OR General only (not both)
            - No education centre network
