🎓 Topic 1 — Work & Study

Do you work or are you a student?

  • What do you enjoy most about your work / studies?

🧠 Topic 2 — Concentration

How easy do you find it to concentrate on a task?

  • What kinds of things distract you when you are trying to concentrate?
  • Do you prefer to work in silence or with background noise?
  • Have you ever taken any steps to help improve your concentration?
📅 Topic 3 — Daily Routine

Can you describe your typical daily routine?

  • Do you prefer to follow a strict routine, or do you like your days to be more flexible?
  • Has your daily routine changed much since you were younger?
  • Do you think having a regular routine is important for productivity?

🤝 Helping Others

Why do you think some people are more willing to help others than others?

🤝 Helping Others

Do you think technology has made it easier or harder for communities to help one another? Why?

Think about: social media, crowdfunding, online support groups.

🌍 Volunteering

How important is volunteering to society, and do you think governments should do more to encourage it?

Consider: tax incentives, school programmes, employer recognition.

🌍 Volunteering

Some people argue that volunteering abroad can do more harm than good. To what extent do you agree?

Think about: dependency, cultural differences, "voluntourism".

🌍 Volunteering

Is it better for young people to spend time volunteering before starting their career, or should they focus on gaining professional experience first?

Consider: skill development, CV value, personal growth.
